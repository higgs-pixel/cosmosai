# Code of Conduct

COSMOS AI is committed to building a respectful, inclusive, and professional community.

## Expected Behavior

All members and contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully
- Give constructive feedback
- Respect different skill levels and backgrounds
- Help beginners learn
- Stay focused on the project mission
- Protect private information and internal work

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not allowed:

- Harassment or bullying
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Sharing private information without consent
- Spamming
- Misusing project access
- Copying or claiming others' work as your own

## Community Standards

COSMOS AI welcomes students, researchers, developers, writers, designers, and space enthusiasts.

We value:

- Curiosity
- Integrity
- Collaboration
- Scientific thinking
- Respect
- Accountability
